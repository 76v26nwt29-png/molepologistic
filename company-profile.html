Company overview

Molepo Logistic Holding is the trading identity of Molepo Logistic Holdings (Pty) Ltd, a South African company operating in the transportation and logistics sector. The business engages with companies, organisations, institutions and individuals on passenger transport, vehicle hire, fleet-related services and practical logistics requirements.

Each request is reviewed according to its scope, route, schedule, passenger or load requirements and operating needs. Written quotations are prepared from the information supplied, and availability and service terms are confirmed directly for each booking.

Service offering

Passenger transportation

Planned journeys and group movements for businesses, institutions and individuals.

Vehicle hire

Toyota HiAce minibus hire enquiries, with or without a driver, subject to availability and terms.

Fleet services

Vehicle coordination and fleet-related support shaped around operational requirements.

Logistics solutions

Practical movement and coordination support for suitable goods and service requirements.

Staff transportation

Enquiries for employee shuttles, recurring routes and workplace transport arrangements.

School & institutional transport

Transport requirements for schools, educational institutions and organised groups.

Events & group transportation

Group movement for events, functions, conferences and planned outings.
