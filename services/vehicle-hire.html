Vehicle hire

Toyota HiAce minibus hire enquiries, with or without a driver.

Service overview

Toyota HiAce minibus hire may be requested with a driver or on a self-drive basis. Availability, hire conditions, driver arrangements and requirements are confirmed during quotation.

Information to include in your enquiry

To help us assess the request and prepare an accurate written quotation, please include:

  • Requested vehicle type and hire period
  • With a driver or self-drive preference
  • Collection and return arrangements
  • Intended use, route and passenger requirements
Availability, operating arrangements and service terms are confirmed individually. A booking is only confirmed once the quotation and applicable terms have been accepted.
