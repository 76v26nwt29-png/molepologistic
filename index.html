Who we are

A practical approach to transportation and logistics.

Molepo Logistics Holdings is a South African company that has operated in the transportation and logistics sector since 2016, from our base in Aeroton, Johannesburg. We recognise the importance of dependable transportation, effective fleet coordination and well-organised logistics in keeping people and businesses moving.

Our approach is centred on understanding each client's requirements and working towards practical, appropriate solutions. We aim to build lasting professional relationships through clear communication, responsible operations and consistent service.

Our missionTo provide practical transport and logistics solutions through professional service and effective coordination.
