About the company

A considered approach to moving people and resources.

Molepo Logistic Holding is the trading identity of Molepo Logistic Holdings (Pty) Ltd, a South African company operating in the transportation and logistics sector. The business works with companies, organisations, institutions and individuals on passenger transport, vehicle hire, fleet-related services and practical logistics requirements.

We start by understanding the scope of each request. Routes, dates, passenger or load requirements and operating arrangements are reviewed before a written quotation is prepared. Availability and service terms are confirmed directly for each booking.
