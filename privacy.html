Information & privacy

Privacy policy

Last reviewed: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Molepo Logistic Holding handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our services. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

About website enquiries: The quotation form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. The website itself does not send or store the form contents. You must review and send the message in WhatsApp for the enquiry to reach us.

Who is responsible?

Molepo Logistic Holdings (Pty) Ltd, trading as Molepo Logistic Holding, is responsible for the personal information you provide in connection with an enquiry. Contact us at info@molepologistic.co.za or +27 84 240 9561.

Information you may provide

Depending on your enquiry, this may include your name, organisation, email address, telephone number, service requested, travel or delivery dates, pickup and destination details, passenger or goods information, and other details you choose to share.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand and assess your request, prepare quotations, communicate with you, arrange services where agreed, and maintain appropriate business records. We do not sell personal information.

WhatsApp and other service providers

If you choose to send an enquiry through WhatsApp, your message is processed by WhatsApp/Meta under its own terms and privacy practices. We may use service providers that support business communications, website hosting or business administration. We do not currently operate an automated WhatsApp assistant through this website.

How long information is kept

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to manage an enquiry or business relationship, and to meet applicable legal or record-keeping obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of record and circumstances.

Security and sharing

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse or unauthorised access. Information may be accessed by people authorised to handle the relevant enquiry and by service providers where necessary to support the business. We do not share enquiry details with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, or raise a concern about how it is handled. Contact info@molepologistic.co.za. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The review date above indicates the latest revision.
